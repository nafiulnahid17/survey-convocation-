ABOUT THIS INITIATIVE

A milestone shaped by student voices.

North Bengal International University’s first convocation is an important milestone in the university community’s journey. It is an opportunity to recognize the hard work, achievements and dedication of graduating students, and to bring students, faculty, families and guests together for a memorable occasion.

This student opinion survey has a focused purpose: to understand students’ preferences regarding the venue for the first convocation, expected to take place on 1 December 2026. Students may indicate whether they prefer the university’s own campus, Zilla Stadium / Convocation Hall, or another suitable location. Those proposing an alternative venue will be asked to name their suggested place in a separate field. A separate optional comments box is available for broader views and suggestions.

Venue choice can affect accessibility, seating capacity, transportation, safety, facilities, cost and the overall experience for students and guests. Gathering feedback in a structured way can help the student community understand preferences and identify the practical considerations that matter most. Responses may be summarized and presented to relevant university representatives for discussion; the survey does not determine the final venue and does not guarantee a particular outcome.

Transparency notice: This is not an official website or official survey of North Bengal International University. It is an independent student initiative organized by the North Bengal International University Students Forum. Participation is voluntary. Please provide accurate information and avoid sharing sensitive personal details.
